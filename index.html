About the village

24 laminated-log houses on parcel 947, Monagroulli (Limassol district, Cyprus): six plots on three flat terraces (plots 6 and 5 at the top, plots 3 and 4 in the middle, plots 1 and 2 with the green area at the bottom, stone retaining walls between them), served by a new internal road, plus a green area with a 10 × 5 m swimming pool by the road, a paddle-tennis court and a lawn with olive and cypress trees.

One house, 24 times. Every house is the owner's hand-drawn two-storey design: a 9.40 × 8.30 m (78 m²) footprint, a double-height living room and kitchen opening onto a south veranda, two bedrooms and a bathroom on the ground floor, and a master bedroom with en-suite and a gallery on the first floor under a 30° roof.

Materials. Walls of 30 cm glulam spruce logs with flat faces and classic crossed corners, exposed glulam rafters and a pine-boarded ceiling, oak floors, a weathered timber deck, anthracite aluminium windows, grey clay roof tiles on a 30° pitch, and concrete plinths on levelled pads.

Model built in Blender, shown with three.js. Use Orbit to look around, Bird's eye for the site plan, Walk to step onto a veranda and into a house.
